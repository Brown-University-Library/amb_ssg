Artist note
Born in Venice 1813, worked in the manner of the earlier Venetian painters. Zona was exhibiting from 1844 till his death. He painted large historical pieces, portraits, and small pictures. Two examples of his work are in the Gallery of Modern Art, Turin.
Description
Two heads, in profile to the right. The nearer girl wears a dark grey-plum-coloured dress and small black bonnet, the further a nun's hood and coif; to the right a pale grey-blue background. Signed " A. Zona."
