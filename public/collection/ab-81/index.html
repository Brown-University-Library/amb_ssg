No. 81. PORTRAIT OF ANNMARY BROWN HAWKINS
Description
Three-quarters length, seated in a red-backed chair. She is dressed in white and holds a lorgnette in her lap. Painted from a photograph. Signed " Seymour Guy, N.Y., 1905." Painted from a photograph taken in New York a few years before Mrs. Hawkins' death in 1903.
