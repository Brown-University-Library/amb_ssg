Artist note
Pieter Neeffs the elder was born probably in 1578 in Antwerp, and probably pupil of H. van Steenwyck. He entered the Antwerp Guild in 1609, and married three years later. The figures in his pictures were usually put in by other painters, such as Vytewael, J. Brueghel, Cocques, Vrancx, and Stalbent. His son, Pieter Neeffs II, was born in 1620. Neeffs the elder died between 1651 and 1656 in Antwerp. He had many imitators. His works are not uncom- mon in the large galleries. He signed his name (as in the example below) "Peeter." Neeffs was rather a draughtsman who coloured accurate perspective drawings than a painter and colourist.
Description
The nave of a large cathedral; a three-arched screen at the end of the vista, in the centre. To the right a pulpit fixed to a pillar. A transept runs across the foreground; on the extreme right the red pillar and architrave of a door. A beggar holding out his hat by a pillar on the right. In the centre two men (one in a scarlet coat), and a boy. Another beggar seated against a pillar to the left, and a man going out by a door on the extreme left. Side chapels seen to left and right. The roof is groined. Signed " Peeter Neeffs."
