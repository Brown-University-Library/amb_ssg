Artist note
Born in Charleston, S.C., in 1826; studied in Dusseldorf, where he lived for some time. He returned to Charleston and settled in New York about 1865. Elected A.N.A. in 1869, and N.A. 1872; Irving died in 1877. Among his works may be mentioned Wine Tasters (1869), Wolsey and his friends (1875), Banquet at Hampton Court in the XVIth Century (1877).
Description
A shaded courtyard. Two cocks fighting in the centre foreground; four priests in the background, beneath a high wall, watching them. Two in scarlet cassocks are standing to left and right; a third in white cassock and black hat and cape seated full face in the centre. Signed " J. Beaufain Irving 1877," and inscribed " Irving N 1."
