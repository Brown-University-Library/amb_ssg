Title as cataloged
No. 82. PORTRAIT OF CARRIE MATHILDE BROWN (SENORA BAJNOTTI)
Description
Leaning on a rail, half length, facing the right front. She wears a black hat trimmed with red violet, a black dress with two red roses at the waist, and a grey silk pleated skirt. Painted from a photograph. Signed " J. S. Guy, 1907." Painted from a photograph taken in Italy.
