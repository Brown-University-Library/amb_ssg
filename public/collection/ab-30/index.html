Artist note
Born about 1700, and at first pupil of Domenico Piola, Francesco de Mura drifted into Solimena's studio (see No. 42). He became his most creditable pupil. Pictures by Mura are common in Naples, and he covered the Palazzo Pubblico at Turin with a frescoed series of the Life of Achilles and the Olympic Games. He also painted portraits.
Description
An angel in blue and white seated in the centre profile left playing the organ. Beyond another beats time. To the left and below in the centre Cherubim with flute and horn and music scores. Above two angels trumpeting down.
