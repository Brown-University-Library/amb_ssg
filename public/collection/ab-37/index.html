Artist note
Pieter Pourbus, son of John Pourbus, was born at Gouda, travelled in Italy, and settled in Bruges. He was in comfortable, even luxurious, circumstances. In 1564 he painted for the magistrates of Bruges a vast panorama of the civic territory. This exists only in a copy. Pieter Pourbus also designed fortifications. He died 1584, leaving a son, FRANCIS POURBUS (1545-1581). He painted religious subjects and portraits. He was taught by his father and by Floris of Antwerp. The name Pourbus is used in a somewhat loose generic way to cover portraits such as the following.
Description
Full face bust portrait of a young woman in a lace coif a la cornette; Dutch or Netherlandish. She wears a black braided dress with puffed sleeves, and an upstanding frilled collar. Inscribed "Aetatis Sue 32, Ano 1572." On the left a coat of arms, a lozenge with three squirrels on a white ground in the upper half; below eight bars on gold.
