Artist note
Asch was born at Delft in 1603 and lived to a great age; his death date is not known. According to Houbraken he was one of the most admired artists of his day. His works are not common; examples are at Delft, Amsterdam, and Copenhagen. He is said to have worked in Wynants' and Ruisdael's manners. His pictures described below show little of Ruisdael's influence, but something of Wynants' in the distant landscape.
Description
In the left foreground a ruined Renaissance palace standing on high ground; before it, in a gleam of sunlight, a woman in a rose-pink skirt holding a baby; a man is kneeling by her side. In the centre mid-distance lies a flat plain, beyond it a castle on the edge of a river. To the right a hill sloping up to the right where stands a palace with a portico. Beyond the river are flat meads backed by low hills in the distance.
