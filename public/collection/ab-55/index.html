Artist note
Studied in Madrid and under Raimundo Madrazo. In 1857 went to Rome. Became Director of the Prado, Madrid. He won medals at Madrid, Munich, Berlin, and Paris in 1862, 1864, and 1890. Two pictures by him are in the Museum of Modern Art, Madrid. He died 1901.
Description
A Roman studio interior; three figures seated round a table. To the right a man in pale rose coat and grey breeches (of the French Revolution period), his right elbow on the table, passing a glass of wine to a woman in scarlet, half reclining on the far side of the table. On her left a black-haired man, his elbows on the table, reading a paper. An easel, canvas, and palette in the right background. Signed " L. Alvarez. Roma, 1886."
