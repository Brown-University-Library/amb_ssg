No. 80. RAPHAEL, JULIUS II, AND CARDINAL BEMBO
Description
The interior of the Pope's cabinet in the Vatican. Julius II seated in the right centre, full face, in crimson cape and cap, and with his feet on a crimson cushion. Cardinal Bembo stands in the centre, profile left, his hat under his arm. Raphael stands on the left, profile right, by his unfinished portrait of the Pope. Behind him a pale blue hanging. Signed " Guerra." Painted in Rome 1877-78. When Julius II was sitting to Raphael (the portrait painted 1510 is now in the Pitti Gallery, Florence, and repetitions are in various galleries, among them the National Gallery, London) Cardinal Bembo was admitted to meet the young painter, whose fame was at its height. When the picture above described was painted in 1877 the papal rooms in the Vatican remained much as they were in 1510; the chair, foot cushion, tapestries, &c., being unchanged.
