No. 89. PORTRAIT OF NICHOLAS BROWN
Description
Grandfather of Annmary Brown Hawkins. Head and shoulders, profile left. In a grey back coat and white stock, with hair nearly white. Painted from a sketch made of Nicholas Brown in church, about 1824. The sitter was not aware of its existence. Born in 1769 Nicholas Brown was noted for his benefactions. In recognition of his munificence to Rhode Island College the name of the College was changed in 1804 to The Brown University. Nicholas Brown died in 1841.
References
Harold E. Dickson, John Wesley Jarvis, (New York Historical Society, 1949) does not include this painting. According to Mr. Jarvis's son who visited the Memorial, this portrait was developed from a sketch made by the artist in church. (Ms. note in a copy of the printed catalog)
