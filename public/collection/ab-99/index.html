No. 99. PORTRAIT OF GENERAL HAWKINS
Artist note
Born in Detroit, trained in Germany and Paris, worked in America, and lives in Holland and Germany.
Description
Seated, half length, profile to the left. He holds a book on his knee. Black coat and tie; the button of the Legion of Honour in the button hole. Grey background. Signed " Gari Melchers, 1908." A portrait of the General aged seventy-seven. Presented to the Memorial by the Artist, 1908.
