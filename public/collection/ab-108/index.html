Artist note
Born in 1824 Trayer studied under his father and Lequien. He was given medals in 1853 and in 1855. He painted genre almost exclusively. His pictures are dated from 1848 to 1886.
Description
A woman seated sewing by an open window. The sunlight falls on her knee. To the left a folding stool; in the background a table and jug of flowers. In 1872 and 1874 Trayer exhibited pictures entitled Seamstresses of Pont-Aven and Seamstresses. The above doubtless belongs to this series.
