Description
An interior. Three people seated in the centre and to the left- two girls and a cavalier. On the right stands the painter and musician holding a lute in profile left. Signed " P. Bouvier, 1877. Milano." Exhibited at Milan in the Triennial Exhibition, 1876. There awarded the Humberto Prize. An example of XIXth Century genre that was in great measure inspired by Meissonier, whose success in 1855, at the French Exhibition, was consummate. This kind of modern genre is yet among the most popular forms of pictures. Salvator Rosa, whose romantic career was equalled by his vogue as a painter, was at first unable to make his mark as an artist. He therefore in 1639 abandoned painting and came out as a poet, singer, and actor. The nature of his improvisation in the above picture can be guessed from the varying expressions of his audience.
