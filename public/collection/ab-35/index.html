Artist note
There were two Patels, pere et fils. The father (b. c. 162O) seems to have signed himself "P. Patel," the son (as in No. 36) "A.P.Patel." " Patel le pere " is said to have worked for Le Sueur, and to have been employed to decorate the apartments of Anne of Austria in the Louvre. He died in 1676. " Patel le fils," sometimes called " Patel le tue," because he was killed in a duel, seems to have been born about 1650. He helped his father decorate the Louvre apartments and is also said to have painted the Twelve Months in St. Louis-la-Culture, Paris. Two pictures signed with his monogram and dated 1705 are at Marseilles. Two etchings by him- Architectural Ruins (presumably akin to the pictures in this Collection) and a View of a Forest- are known. His style closely followed his father's, so that their works are generally confused. Owing to the clear signature on No. 36 in this Memorial, it is clear that these examples are by Patel le fils or "tue."
Description
On the right the columns of a ruined palace over- grown with trees. In the centre a woman riding on an ass, a man walking behind. A tall tree on the extreme left. Below in the mid-distance a wooded plain extending to blue distant hills. A glowing sunset sky.
References
