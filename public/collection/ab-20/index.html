No. 20. INNOCENCE
Description
A young woman with bare torso leaning her right elbow on her blue mantle:-in front of her and caressing two white doves. She is seated nearly half length, looking slightly to the right. Behind her on the left background a bacchanal with gleaming eyes. Sky background. An allegory of the blindness of virtue or the helplessness of Ignorance.
