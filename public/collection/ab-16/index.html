Title as cataloged
No. 16. PORTRAIT OF A LADY
Description
Head and shoulders, looking to the left front. Her hands, one covering the other, rest on a book to the left. She has dark brown hair braided over her ears, and deep hazel eyes. Her black cape is lined with brown fur; her sleeves are light brown. Over her forehead is a gold band under a fine transparent linen coif, the ends of which fall low on her breast in two points. She wears six rings. Clear moss-green background. Formerly assigned to Holbein; from a Collection near Grenoble. A somewhat similar portrait, as regards type and structure, assigned to Bartholomaeus Bruyn, the elder, was in the Albert Bossy Collection, Paris (1904). (See Les Arts, No. 35, p. 14.) But clearly the painter of this picture is distinct from the painter of No. 15 (above).
