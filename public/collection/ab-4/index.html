Artist note
This school, largely directed throughout its course by the Carracci, is best known by its eclectic principles and by its most popular exponents- Guido, Domenichino, Albani, &c. Lodovico Carracci's eclecticism embraced Andrea Del Sarto the Florentine, Titian the Venetian, and Correggio the Ferrarese.
Description
The Angel dressed in brown-rose is seen nearly half length walking to the right, and looking down to the left at the boy Tobit, who looks up at him. The Angel holds a small lamp (?) in his right hand.
