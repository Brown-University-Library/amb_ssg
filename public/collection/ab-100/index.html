No. 100. THE FIRST TOOTH, NEW YEAR'S DAY
Artist note
Born at Cambrai; studied in Paris under Gerome. First exhibiting 1868. Was exhibiting in 1886. Awarded a third class Medal 1884.
Description
A young mother in pale apple-green and Cambridge blue seated on the steps of a house, her baby in her lap, wrapped in a quilt of pale grey-rose. To the right a lemon-yellow cushion. Below two steel-grey pigeons. To the left, dressed in rose silk, a man bends to admire the child's tooth, which the mother is pointing out. Signed " E. Pinchart." Painted in 1878. A good example of the Gerome Anecdotic School and of the costume genre of the XIXth Century. This picture is cited in The Cyclopaedia of Painters and Paintings (Scribners). It was one of Pinchart's most popular pieces.
