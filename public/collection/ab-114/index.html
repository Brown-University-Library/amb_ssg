Description
Milking Time. Landscape with a luminous golden sky; in the foreground are several cows; the one standing, center, is being milked. At the right a sleeping boy is being tickled by a lad who is half-hidden behind a tree. At the left, mid-distance, is a bridge from which two men watch two fishermen below. By a close follower of Albert Cuyp, whose paintings have often passed for the work of that master. Signed: "J. Van Stry."
