Artist note
Jan Both was born probably in Utrecht and studied under his father, a painter on glass, and Abraham Bloemart. With his elder brother Andries he visited Italy, and his landscape is in consequence completely italianate. He was Master of the Utrecht Guild in 1649, and buried there in 1652. Both's influence was wide. He is best known for the soft golden quality of his light and atmosphere. Pictures by him are numerous in the public galleries. In the hands of the italianising Dutch painters, who having travelled in Italy then settled in Holland and continued painting more or less imaginary Italian scenes, the Dutch landscape school became conventional and mannered.
Description
To the right a high mass of buildings, ruins at the top. Below, in sunlight, a packhorse with a red bundle on his back, and to the right a child holding the reins, a woman with a fruit basket on her shoulders and a man seated pulling up his stockings. In the left background an archway and open timber gate. The sky is golden and pale turquoise in the zenith. Signed " Both. f."
