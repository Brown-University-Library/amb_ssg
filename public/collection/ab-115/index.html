Description
This interesting composition by an unknown artist may have been the work of Paolo Veronese (Caliari), made as a study for a larger painting, not long before his decease in 1588. Its richness of color scheme and artistic composition point to an accomplished artist, and to the School of Venice more than to any other in Italy. Signed: " P. C."
