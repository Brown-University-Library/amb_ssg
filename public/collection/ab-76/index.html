Title as cataloged
No. 76. VETHEUIL, SEINE-ET-OISE
Description
French pastoral. The village standing in the centre background, in late sunlight. In the foreground, in shadow, the still river. Boats moored to the far bank. Signed " Camille Dufour." Exhibited 1904, Paris Salon. Painted in the direct tradition of the Barbizon School, especially in the vein of Daubigny and Harpignies.
