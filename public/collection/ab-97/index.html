No. 97. FAIRY STORY
Artist note
Born in Detroit, trained in Germany and Paris, worked in America, and lives in Holland and Germany.
Description
A girl in an orange terra-cotta cape seated on the right, profile left, looking down. At her knee, looking up at her, stands a little boy in a rich blue tunic and white sleeves. Beyond them a parapet; on the top a lemon-yellow vase with pinks; a painted conventional landscape background. Signed " Gari Melchers." Typical of the modern German School, halfway between the Impressionists and the Post-Impressionists. This picture and the following are good illustrations of pictorial conditions observed (see under Leclerq, No. 95)
