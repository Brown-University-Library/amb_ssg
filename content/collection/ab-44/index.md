description = "Mary is seated on the right, full length three-quarters to the left, holding her Son naked on her right knee. She is dressed in dark brown-red and Antwerp blue. The little Christ is seated three-quarters left, raising His right hand in blessing.  Before Mary, kissing the Infant's right foot, kneels Melchior, the oldest of the Kings, in ermine and vermilion. A chalice, his gift, and his green cap, are in the paved court below Mary. Immediately above Melchior stands Balthasar, a gold cup in his left hand, his right arm in its rich golden sleeve held up across his body. He looks to the left, where stands Jaspar, the African King of Saba, in profile right, also bearing a gold chalice in his right hand.  In the background a ruined archway, rocks on the left, and distant mountains in the centre and on the right. Behind Mary a vermilion curtain with an effect of sunlight and shadow; the Star above her head.\n\n This is by a painter of the XVIth Century Flemish School. The type of the Virgin and of the Child suggest that the artist was Spanish. On the other hand, the influence of Quentin Massys and in especial of Mabuse is very strong. A somewhat similar picture showing the same prevailing influences is in the\n National Gallery, London, inscribed \" Lo Fil De Mestre Rodriguez.\" The influence of Mabuse was very much felt in Spain and Portugal in the XVIth Century."
