catalog_note = "Adriaen, the elder brother and instructor of Isack van Ostade, was born at and lived in Haarlem all his life. His first master was Frans Hals, whose influence is not strongly reflected in his work. His earlier pieces represent scenes of drinking and dancing, and introduce effects of sudden, even crude lighting and violent action. In these early works he painted many figures. After 1640 his pictures become more reticent and harmonious, with subtle effects of tone, fewer even single figures. He also attempted some out-of-doors pieces and a few portraits. From about 1647 to 1670 he was at his best. Later his painting became small and polished in execution and in colour rather gaudy. He is admirably represented in Amsterdam, Berlin, London, The Hague, Paris, Dresden, and Vienna. A. van Ostade died in Haarlem 1685. His brother Isack, a painter of rare charm, died when only twenty-eight; his earliest work is often confused with that of Adriaen."
