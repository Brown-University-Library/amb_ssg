catalog_note = "Patrick Nasmyth, son of Alexander Nasmyth, was born in Edinburgh, and taught by his father. He went  o London about 1807: from 1811 he exhibited at the Royal Academy. Having to a large extent lost the use of his right hand he had to learn to paint with his left. His best landscapes have a breadth and sense of atmosphere that his later works miss in reproducing an abundance of small detail. Nasmyth died in South Lambeth, 1831. He is well represented in the Edinburgh National Gallery and in the London Gallery. His work is not often as luminous and golden in tone as is the example described below."
