He began to exhibit in 1776, thus being a competitor with Reynolds, Gainsborough, and Romney. From about 1783 until 1786 Beechey lived in Norwich. In 1793 he became an Associate of the Royal Academy (one year after Reynolds' death, five after Gainsborough's) and painter to the Queen. In 1798 he was elected a full member, and knighted. He continued to exhibit until his death in 1839. Portraits by Beechey are in the National Gallery, London, the Louvre, the National Portrait Gallery, London, and Kensington Palace.
