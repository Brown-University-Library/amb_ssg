Signed \" T. Couture.\"\n\n A picture of Couture's less academic style, and one in closer touch with more modern tendencies. Couture is notorious for his attempted suppression of Marnet's development. Manet, the great influence of the XIXth Century and one of its permanent masters, studied under Couture, who stood in the ranks of the Classicists. Manet's tendency to revolt from semi-classic historical painting and to paint the world as he saw it disturbed Couture. But before such a picture as the above we might think it almost inexplicable that Couture should have found himself at variance with Manet's ambitions. Indeed, there is in this picture something of the Manet influence."
